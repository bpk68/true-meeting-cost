## Design Context

### Users

Team leads, facilitators, and cost-conscious organizations who need to understand the financial impact of meetings while planning them or while they run.

### Brand Personality

Clear, candid, energetic. The experience should make an invisible cost tangible without shaming users.

### Aesthetic Direction

A warm editorial utility: paper-toned surfaces, ink typography, vermilion accents, and oversized numeric readouts. Light-first, calm, and deliberately unlike a corporate analytics dashboard.

### Design Principles

- Make the live cost unmistakable and readable at a glance.
- Keep setup fast while supporting realistic compensation differences.
- Use plain language and progressive disclosure rather than jargon.
- Treat accessibility, keyboard use, responsive layouts, and reduced motion as core behavior.
- Use color and motion only to communicate state.
